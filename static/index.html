A tumbling-ship clock for the ESP32-2432S028 "Cheap Yellow Display"

Plug your CYD in with a USB data cable, pick your board below and click Connect. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer. After flashing, enter your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi details, then Home Assistant will discover the clock automatically.

Dual USB (USB-C + micro-USB) — ST7789V

The most common dual-USB board. If the colours look like a photo negative, try the ILI9342 build.

Dual USB (USB-C + micro-USB) — ILI9342

Some dual-USB boards ship with an ILI9342 panel instead.

Original (micro-USB only) — ILI9341

The first-generation CYD with a single micro-USB socket.
